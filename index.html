How Sideloading Works on this Site (No Lies/Fake Animations):

iOS Sideloading via Safari relies on Apple's standard itms-services://?action=download-manifest&url=HTTPS_MANIFEST_URL protocol. To install without a PC:

  • The Target IPA must be signed with a valid Apple Enterprise Certificate or custom provisioning profile.
  • The manifest PLIST must be served strictly over an HTTPS endpoint.
  • After tapping SIDELOAD NOW, go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management to Trust the installed profile!
